# Rules and evidence

The goal is economic control of Blockreach's resources. Coinfall and Freehold,
regional holds and actual received connections define the geography. Independent
factions have no third issued currency; accepting GOLD/DQS does not imply alliance,
annexation or permission to debit someone else's balance.

Only validated deposits introduce token credit. Personal custody balances, royal
treasury, merchant budgets, household purses, reservations and external wallet
balances are different accounts. Taxes and trade redistribute finite money.
Food must be produced, stocked, sold and consumed. No passive camp mint and no
currency debit that creates food. Orders reserve real funds; caravans reserve
stock, workers and provisions. Accepted contracts survive a crown transfer.

One persistent clock continues production, consumption, danger and trade without
humans present. Arrival may change detail, not geography, ownership or history.
AI kings cannot annex kingdoms or take crowns through a victory shortcut. Only
humans conquer kingdoms. Crown ownership is now an account-held off-chain item;
legacy helmet-equipment behavior is not its authority source.
